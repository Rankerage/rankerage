What is Rankerage?

Rankerage.com is a living database of world rankings. We track 269 metrics across 202 countries, 48 global companies, 55 notable individuals, and 34 international organizations — 339 entries in total.

What can you do here?

🔍 Search any ranking — from GDP to FIFA, from coffee consumption to Nobel prizes.
📊 Click column headers to sort by any metric — instantly compare countries.
🔥 See what's trending in real-time news — our trending ticker shows who's making headlines.
🌐 Click any country to see its best rankings, or explore by region.

Data Sources

Our data comes from reputable international sources including the World Bank, IMF, UN, WHO, UNESCO, CIA Factbook, IOC, FIFA, Transparency International, and many more. We update data continuously to keep rankings current.

Our Mission

We believe data should be beautiful, fast, and addictive. Rankerage turns dry statistics into an interactive playground where you can discover surprising facts — like which country drinks the most coffee, who has the fastest internet, or which company is bigger than most countries.
